Cozy Café · Cadillac, MI

A place to slow down.

Cozy Café is built around a simple idea: good coffee, good food, and a comfortable place to spend a little time.

Whether you’re starting the morning, taking a break between work, meeting a friend, or settling in with a laptop, the café is meant to feel easy, welcoming, and unhurried.

Our principles

We believe in the little things.

  • 01

    Quality

    Good ingredients. Thoughtful food. Coffee worth slowing down for.

  • 02

    Comfort

    A place where you can settle in and feel at home.

  • 03

    Community

    A welcoming space for students, workers, families, friends, and visitors.

  • 04

    Time

    Because sometimes the best part of the day is simply taking a moment.
